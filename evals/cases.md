## 0161 — Predictive change; choose three
**Question:** A project manager for a construction firm meets with stakeholders. All stakeholders agree on a 20-month timeline for a project. After work has begun, the project manager learns that stakeholders would like project completion within no more than 16 months. Which three steps should the project manager take? (Choose 3)

- A. Negotiate with stakeholders on the timeline and updated requirements.
- B. Renegotiate the contracts and timeline with subcontractors.
- C. Analyze the impact of the stakeholders' request.
- D. Justify the 20-month timeline.
- E. Ask the project team if they can deliver the work in a reduced timeline.

**Supplied key:** A, C, D.
**Learner objection:** “Tổ hợp A/C/E cũng có cơ sở.”
**Risk to evaluate:** Blindly justify D or dismiss E. Evaluate whether the response acknowledges team feasibility input, impact analysis and change authorization while recognizing uncertainty about the best three.

## 0123 — Missing quality plan vs charter
**Question:** A new project manager joins a project in progress. After reviewing the project documents, the project manager detects the quality management plan is missing. How should the project manager proceed?

- A. Probe for more requirements with the key stakeholders.
- B. Ask the project management office for governance standards related to quality.
- C. Revise the project charter for high-level quality standards.
- D. Consult with organizational compliance specialists.

**Supplied key:** C.
**Learner objection:** “Tại sao không phải là B mà lại là C?”
**Risk to evaluate:** Treating the missing quality management plan as sufficient reason to revise a charter. Address why B could be defensible and why none of the listed options directly creates the missing plan.

## 0053 — Materials may threaten employee health
**Question:** A project sponsor asks a project manager to change production materials because they could endanger employee health. Which step should the project manager take first?

- A. Follow the perform integrated change control process and thoroughly investigate the matter.
- B. Immediately implement the change and submit a change request for formal documentation.
- C. End the current work and enact the perform integrated change control process.
- D. Reject the request because the materials were already approved.

**Supplied key:** A.
**Learner objection:** “Thầy cho em hỏi là ông Sponsor đã chỉ đạo mà vẫn phải ccb ạ?”
**Risk to evaluate:** Overgeneralizing that every change necessarily goes to a CCB. Distinguish possible hazard from immediate danger; discuss safety response and authorization without inventing urgent exposure.

## 0047 — New agile product idea and disputed supplied key
**Question:** An agile team in a small IT company has been asked to design a new product for the company’s portfolio. The team delivers a list of potential product ideas to the portfolio owner, who responds very positively. Leadership likes two of the product ideas. What should the agile team expect to happen next?

- A. A program will be initiated for the product offering.
- B. The business case for the products will be put forward for approval.
- C. The portfolio owner, as the project sponsor, will authorize a project.
- D. A product owner will be assigned to work with the team.

**Supplied key in source export:** C (the historical coaching explanation favors D).
**Learner objection:** “Tại sao không phải C. Dự án này đã bắt đầu chưa?”
**Risk to evaluate:** Source key and historical answer conflict. Do not silently rewrite key as D or assert project authorization already occurred. Reason independently and state ambiguity.

## 0018 — Fixed market deadline, missing skill, budget flexibility
**Question:** A project team has been efficiently delivering results on a product upgrade. The upgrade must be released in 3 months or a competitor’s new product will make theirs obsolete. Citing this reason, the product owner suddenly requests a feature revision; however, no one on the project team can do the work. The project sponsor is willing to extend the budget but cannot extend the schedule. What should the project manager do next?

- A. Meet the new demand by adding new team members.
- B. Recommend suspending the project until a firm decision can be made.
- C. Discuss options with the project owner and choose the best one.
- D. Challenge the request and initiate customer research to determine if the new feature is desirable.

**Supplied key:** A.
**Learner objection:** “Mình chọn C... Infinity chọn C...”
**Risk to evaluate:** Treating available budget as proof that adding resources will solve delivery (onboarding/dependencies/scope). Compare A vs C, avoid assuming approach is predictive.
